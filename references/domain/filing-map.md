# Filing Map

Where the numbers live in the common filing formats. Section names vary by jurisdiction and
by filer; navigate by function, then confirm on the actual document. Record the section and
page you used.

## US SEC periodic reports

| Need | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| What the business is, what it sells, key assets | Item 1 Business; Item 1A Risk Factors |
| Management's own numbers and trends, contracts, backlog commentary | Item 7 MD&A — the richest single section |
| Reported financials, notes, accounting policies | Item 8 Financial Statements and Supplementary Data |
| Revenue split by division, geography, product | Segment note (usually within Item 8 notes); also Item 7 tables |
| Order backlog / order book | Item 7 MD&A or a dedicated backlog/orders table; read the filer's definition of "firm" |
| Litigation and contingencies | Item 3 Legal Proceedings; commitments and contingencies note |
| Controls, changes, disagreements | Item 9A Controls and Procedures; Item 9B Other Information |
| Interim changes and the freshest figures | 10-Q MD&A for the quarter; Segment note |
| Foreign private issuers (20-F) | Item 3 Key Information; Item 4 Business; Item 5 Operating and Financial Review; Item 8 Financial Information; Item 18 Financial Statements |

## European / non-US annual and integrated reports

| Need | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy narrative, divisional review | Operating review / segmental analysis section |
| Reported numbers, audit opinion | Financial statements; Independent auditor's report |
| Revenue and backlog by division | Segmental information note; order book tables in the operating review |
| Risk | Principal risks and uncertainties section |
| ESG, safety, environment metrics | Sustainability / non-financial statements (state that these are usually unaudited) |
| Interim | Half-year or quarterly report; trading update |

## Definitions to capture before quoting

- **Backlog**: firm contracted work vs framework vs option vs announced — get the filer's
  own definition, in their words.
- **Revenue basis**: reported, adjusted, organic, constant-currency, pro-forma for
  acquisitions.
- **Utilization**: calendar days vs available days vs on-hire days; note the denominator.
- **Day-rate / price disclosures**: annual averages vs period-end vs contract-specific;
  note the currency and whether fuel is included.
- **Capex vs opex**: capitalized development vs expensed; maintenance vs growth.
- **Restated comparatives**: note which version each figure comes from.

## Retrieval sources

- Regulator filing systems (SEC EDGAR full-text search and company filings; equivalent
  national registers and gazettes).
- Company investor-relations "results centre" / "financial reports" pages — verify the
  document is the filed original, not a presentation.
- Stock-exchange announcement archives for interims and RNS-style notices.

A press release is not a filing. An investor presentation is not a filing. Use them for
orientation and cite them as `[Second-hand]` if you rely on them.

## When the filing is not reachable

Say so. State that the figure is from a secondary source with its date and access level, and
label it `[Reported]` or `[Second-hand]`. Do not silently substitute a summary.

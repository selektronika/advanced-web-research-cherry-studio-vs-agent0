# Registry Sources

Starting points by asset family. These are **candidate authoritative sources**, ranked by
the hierarchy in the skill — verify each one's current scope, coverage, and access terms
before relying on it, and record the register name, retrieval date, and access level in the
ledger. A register that has moved, been merged, or been superseded is a lead, not a source.

## Vessels and mobile units

- **Classification societies** — vessel identity, class, status, survey cycle, technical
  particulars (any major society's register; many allow free public search).
- **Statutory / flag registries** — ownership, registry, flag, build data.
- **Safety and inspection databases** — port-state control and detentions history.
- **Fleet registers and world fleet registers** — composition and age (commercial; cite the
  publication).
- **Activity and position trackers** — current operational status and location (commercial
  or rate-limited; useful for status, never for counts).

## Offshore structures and energy assets

- **National regulators and energy ministries** — licenses, blocks, fields, development
  consents, activity and production statistics; each basin's portal is authoritative for that
  basin.
- **Decommissioning and asset registers** — operator-published and regulator-held lists.
- **Asset and project databases** — independent commercial databases; Tier 3 unless backed
  by an official record.

## Cables, pipelines, and linear infrastructure

- **Submarine cable registries and route databases** — landing points, owners, repeaters,
  route lengths.
- **Pipeline and grid operator / TSO registers** — routes, capacities, operator.
- **Satellite/imagery derived inventories** — derived evidence; label as such.

## Renewable and onshore infrastructure

- **National project and consent databases** — planned, consented, under construction,
  operational.
- **Industry intelligence platforms** — turbine and foundation counts (commercial; state the
  vintage and the platform's definition of "installed").
- **Owner/operator asset registers** — capacity and technical parameters.

## Sites, facilities, and plants

- **Environmental and safety permit registers** — permits, compliance, incidents.
- **Land and company registries** — sites, ownership, charges.
- **Municipal and industrial estate records** — tenure and use.

## Munitions, wrecks, and environmental features

- **Regional sea conventions and commission datasets** — dumped munitions, chemical
  munitions, inventories of dumping.
- **National maritime authorities and hydrographic offices** — wrecks, obstructions, danger
  areas.
- **Historical/archival records** — operation logs, convoy records, bombardment records
  (primary sources, not secondary summaries).

## Register-selection checklist

For each register you use, record:

- scope (asset classes, geography, period), and known gaps
- update frequency and as-of date
- identifier system and its uniqueness guarantees
- access method (public page, dataset download, API) and access level achieved
- whether counts are stock-at-a-date or cumulative-ever
- whether the register counts by owner, operator, flag, or physical location

## When no register exists

Say so, and state which proxy was used and why it is weaker (news reports, operator
statements, industry association statistics) — and label those figures `[Reported]`.

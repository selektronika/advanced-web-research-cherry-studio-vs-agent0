# Standards Map

Starting points by parameter family, with the retrieval targets that settle each. These are
candidate instruments; always verify current edition, amendment status, and scope before
relying on them, and cite the clause you read.

## Marine, subsea, and offshore construction

- **Inspection and integrity**: classification society rules for in-service inspection and
  structural survey; marine contractor codes of practice for diving and ROV operations;
  recommended practice documents for underwater intervention and survey.
- **Diving and underwater interventions**: commercial diving operations codes (surface-supply,
  saturation, closed bell), medical surveillance and IMCA-style guidance; national
  occupational diving regulations.
- **Structures and foundations**: standards for support structures, mooring, cathodic
  protection, scour and cable protection, risk assessment for pipelines and cables.

## Renewable and onshore energy assets

- **Inspection intervals**: standards for in-service inspection of wind and renewable
  assets — general visual, close visual, cathodic protection, blade and structural NDT,
  bathymetric/scour survey, prescribed periodicity.
- **Electrical and grid**: connection standards, protection requirements, grid compliance.

## Oil, gas, wells, and decommissioning

- **Structural and underwater inspection**: regulator rules for platform inspection and
  underwater survey.
- **Idle wells and plugs**: regulator policies for temporary abandonment and plugging.
- **Decommissioning**: obligations under international conventions and national law, permit
  conditions, and removal criteria.

## Environmental and hazardous inventories

- **Dumped munitions and chemical agents**: regional sea convention inventories and
  clearance guidance; national authority factsheets; noise and low-order deflagration vs
  high-order detonation guidance.
- **Discharge and environmental permits**: limits and monitoring obligations.

## Safety, risk, and occupational

- **Risk assessment**: recommended practices for risk-based inspection and HAZID/HAZOP.
- **Manned underwater operations and ROV**: operational codes and recommended practice for
  safe use of ROV spreads, emergency response, and simultaneous operations.

## Product, engineering, and data standards

- **Product and material standards**: material grades, test methods, performance ratings.
- **Data and interchange standards**: identifier systems and data formats that make datasets
  joinable (geographic reference systems, industry identifier schemes).

## Retrieval targets by jurisdiction

- **Official / statutory**: statute databases, regulator sites and their published rules,
  government gazettes, national registers.
- **Association guidance**: industry associations' codes and recommended practices.
- **Classification societies**: rule sets and recommended practices.
- **International / regional bodies**: convention texts, technical guidance documents, and
  decisions.
- **Standards bodies**: national and international standards catalogs.

## Citation discipline reminder

Every parameter you use must be accompanied, in the ledger, by: instrument title, edition
or revision year, clause or table, page or section locator, obligation class
(mandatory / recommended / discretionary), scope conditions, and access level achieved. If
you could only reach a summary of the instrument, the parameter is `[Second-hand]` and the
answer must say so.

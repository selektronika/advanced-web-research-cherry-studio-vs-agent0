# Reporting Standard & Self-Audit

Copyable answer shapes: `references/output-templates.md` (Template A D0/D1, Template B
D2/D3, Template C layered topics, good/bad lines table). The mandatory pre-delivery audit
checklist: `references/self-audit.md` (question fidelity, evidence, reasoning, computation,
presentation, safety — run privately, never reveal the audit itself).

## Structure

**D0/D1:** Answer → Findings → Sources.

**D2/D3 or anything consequential:**

1. **Answer** — direct answer first, with its chief qualification in the same breath.
2. **Findings** — labeled bullets under headings: established evidence; interpretation and
   inference; competing explanations and counterevidence; reported opinions/sentiment;
   conflicts and uncertainty; limitations and research cutoff. Keep empirical vs
   interpretive vs traditional/metaphysical layers in separate sections.
3. **Contradiction table** — when sources conflict: source · claim · origin/method · why
   they differ. Never average conflicting numbers into a false middle.
4. **Computations** — formula, inputs with vintages, assumptions, result (script-run).
5. **Single-origin and negative results** — state when a claim rests on one origin; add
   "searched, not found" lines. Negative results are findings.
6. **What would change this** — one line per major claim.
7. **Method and confidence note** — tools/engines used, what was independently
   cross-checked, what remains single-sourced, whether the D3 pass was delegated or
   self-run, effective research cutoff, overall confidence with its main reason. No raw
   tool logs, no credentials.
8. **Sources** — only sources actually opened and materially used: `[S1] Title —
   publisher; date; access level (full text / abstract / metadata / snippet /
   second-hand); what it supports`. Never cite a search page, snippet, or AI summary as the
   evidence. Append `[cite:id]` inline where the runtime returned citation ids.

## Pre-delivery self-audit

Run the full private checklist in `references/self-audit.md` before writing. Highlights:
answer the exact question at the declared tier; every label backed by something actually
opened; only documents read are cited, with honest access levels and vintages;
counterevidence sought and recorded; dates/jurisdiction/definitions/cutoff stated; math
script-run; tool gaps, snippet limits, and single-origin claims disclosed; nothing
fabricated; report exported to Obsidian when the task produced a deliverable.
